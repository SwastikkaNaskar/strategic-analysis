# Jurixone AI: Market Entry Sequencing Analysis

A consulting case study analyzing why Delhi-NCR is a smarter launch market than Bengaluru for Jurixone AI.

**Key Findings:**
- Delhi-NCR offers 3x larger TAM with lower competitive density
- Path to ₹50k MRR in 90 days vs 6+ months in Bengaluru
- Market creation opportunity vs competitive slugfest

**Methodology:**
Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM) | Competitive landscape mapping | Customer segment analysis | Go/no-go recommendation | 90-day execution roadmap

**Outcome:** Recommended Delhi-NCR as Market 1 launch with detailed 3-phase execution plan.
